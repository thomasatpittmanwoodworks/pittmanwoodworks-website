About Pittman Woodworks

The people behind every cabinet we build.

Pittman Woodworks is a family-owned commercial casework shop in North Richland Hills, Texas. Our estimators, drafters, shop crew and installers work as one team, from the first drawing to the final punch list.

Our story

A shop built on doing what we say

Pittman Woodworks started with a simple idea: commercial clients deserve a cabinet shop that communicates clearly, prices honestly and delivers on schedule.

Led by co-owners Seth Pittman and Eric Jorgenson, we've grown into a full-service casework company serving schools, healthcare facilities, restaurants, retailers and offices across North Texas. We combine the personal attention of a family business with the systems and equipment of a much larger operation.

Most of our projects come from repeat customers and referrals. We think that says more about our work than anything we could write here.

HonestyClear, truthful communication with every client, at every stage of the job.
TransparencyDetailed pricing, material specifications and realistic timelines, so there are no surprises.
ExcellenceQuality materials, careful craftsmanship and service that earns the next project.
