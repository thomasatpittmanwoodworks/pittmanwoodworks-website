Our commitment

Pittman Woodworks wants everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use our website, learn about our work, request a bid and apply for a job. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

What we’ve done

  • Clear headings and page structure that work with screen readers.
  • Text and backgrounds chosen for readable color contrast, in both light and dark display modes.
  • Every form field has a visible label, and errors are explained in plain language.
  • The Site can be used with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline.
  • Animations are reduced automatically for visitors who turn on “reduce motion” in their device settings.
  • Layouts adjust for phones, tablets and desktop screens, and support browser zoom.

Known limitations

Some content, such as uploaded project photos and drawings, may not yet have complete text descriptions. We are working to improve this as we add new content.

Need help or found a problem?

If you have trouble using any part of the Site, or need information in another format, please contact us and we will help:

  • Phone: (469) 375-0495
  • Email: office@pittmanwoodworks.com

Please tell us the page and what you were trying to do. We aim to respond within two business days.
