    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    Q.01Do you charge for estimates?

    No. Estimates and site visits are provided at no cost, with an itemized breakdown of the proposed scope.

    Q.02Can you bid from architect's plans?

    Yes. General contractors send us drawings and specs every week. We bid from the plans, then produce shop drawings and submittals for approval before anything is cut.

    Q.03Do you install, or just build?

    Both. Our own crew installs everything we build, including countertops, and handles the punch list with you at the end.

    Q.04Do you do residential work?

    Commercial is our focus. We do take on multifamily projects for builders and general contractors. For a single-family home, give us a call and we'll tell you straight whether we're the right fit.

    Q.05What area do you serve?

    The Dallas–Fort Worth area and surrounding North Texas. Our shop is in North Richland Hills. If you're farther out, call us and ask.
