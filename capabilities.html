Company overview

Pittman Woodworks is a family-owned commercial casework and countertop subcontractor in North Richland Hills, Texas. We engineer shop drawings, CNC-fabricate in our own shop and install with our own crews, giving general contractors one accountable partner from bid through closeout.

Core capabilities

  • Plastic laminate commercial casework
  • Plastic laminate, solid surface and quartz countertops
  • Frameless 32 mm and face-frame construction
  • Built to AWS Economy, Custom or Premium grade when specified
  • Accessible (ADA / TAS) casework and countertops
  • Shop drawings and full submittal packages
  • Self-performed installation and punch list

How we deliver

  • Field verification before drawings are finalized
  • Shop drawings engineered in Cabinet Vision
  • CNC machining with barcode part tracking
  • Pre-delivery inspection on every order
  • Single point of contact from bid through closeout
  • Bid requests answered within one business day
