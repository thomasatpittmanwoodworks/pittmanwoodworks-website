Commercial Cabinet Installer FieldFull-timeDFW job sites

Install commercial casework and countertops on schools, healthcare, restaurant and office projects across North Texas.

What you'll do

  • Set, level, scribe and fasten cabinets to shop drawings
  • Install countertops, hardware and trim
  • Work punch lists and walk finished work with the general contractor
  • Keep job sites clean, safe and on schedule

What you bring

  • Experience installing cabinets or finish carpentry
  • Own hand tools and reliable transportation
  • Valid driver’s license
  • Able to read shop drawings and lift 75 lbs

Cabinet Assembler ShopFull-timeNorth Richland Hills shop

Build commercial cabinet boxes, drawers and doors from CNC-cut parts in our North Richland Hills shop.

What you'll do

  • Assemble cabinets to shop drawings and cut lists
  • Install hinges, slides and hardware
  • Scan parts and log progress at your station
  • Check work for square, fit and finish

What you bring

  • Cabinet or millwork shop experience preferred
  • Comfortable with pneumatic and power tools
  • Attention to detail and accurate measuring
  • Able to stand and lift through a full shift

CNC & Machine Operator ShopFull-timeNorth Richland Hills shop

Run our CNC router and edgebander, keeping parts moving accurately to assembly.

What you'll do

  • Load programs and sheet goods and run the CNC
  • Label parts and sort them for assembly
  • Run the edgebander and keep machines maintained
  • Verify part sizes against cut lists

What you bring

  • CNC router or panel-processing experience preferred
  • Basic computer skills
  • Mechanical aptitude and a careful, safe approach
  • Willing to be trained on our equipment

Production Helper ShopFull-timeNorth Richland Hills shop

Entry-level role supporting every station in the shop. No experience required, and a clear path into assembly, machining or installation.

What you'll do

  • Stage materials and move parts between stations
  • Help with assembly, edgebanding and packing
  • Load trucks for delivery
  • Keep the shop clean and organized

What you bring

  • Reliable, on time and ready to learn
  • Able to lift 50 lbs and stand for a full shift

  • Interest in learning a trade
