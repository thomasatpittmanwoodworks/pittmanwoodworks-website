    Construction method

    Built to your specification

    We build to the construction type, grade and materials in your project documents, including Architectural Woodwork Standards (AWS) grades when specified.

    Our standard

    Frameless construction

    European-style, 32 mm system casework with full-overlay doors and drawer fronts. Clean lines, maximum interior space and consistent, CNC-bored hardware locations.

    • Doors cover the cabinet box edges
    • 32 mm line boring for shelves and hardware
    • Ideal for healthcare, education and office work
    Available

    Face-frame construction

    A solid frame across the front of the cabinet box, for projects that specify a traditional look or added front rigidity.

    • Hardwood frame on the cabinet front
    • Overlay or inset doors
    • Common in hospitality and feature millwork

    Quality

    Seven checkpoints on every job

    Quality isn't a final inspection. It's built into each step, from the first site visit to the last punch item.

    1. 01
      Field verification

      We measure the site and confirm walls, openings, utilities and blocking before drawings are finalized.

    2. 02
      Engineered shop drawings

      Every cabinet is drawn in Cabinet Vision, with plans, elevations and sections, and approved before fabrication.

    3. 03
      CNC machining

      Parts are cut and bored on the CNC directly from the approved drawings, so every part matches the design.

    4. 04
      Part tracking

      Each part is labeled and scanned as it moves through edgebanding, assembly and finishing.

    5. 05
      Assembly checks

      Cabinets are checked for square, fit, hardware function and finish before they leave the bench.

    6. 06
      Pre-delivery inspection

      Each order is verified complete against the job before it is loaded, so nothing is missing on install day.

    7. 07
      Installation & punch list

      Our crews install, adjust and clean, then walk the work with you and close out every punch item.
