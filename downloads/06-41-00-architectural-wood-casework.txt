PART 1 – GENERAL

1.1 SUMMARY
  A. Section includes: Plastic laminate faced architectural wood casework, cabinet hardware, and [plastic laminate countertops].
  B. Related requirements: Section 06 10 00 Rough Carpentry for in-wall blocking; Section 12 36 00 Countertops for [solid surface] [quartz] countertops; Division 22 for sinks and faucets.

1.2 REFERENCES
  A. Architectural Woodwork Standards (AWS), current edition, published by AWI, AWMAC and WI.
  B. ANSI A208.1 Particleboard; ANSI A208.2 Medium Density Fiberboard (MDF).
  C. NEMA LD 3 High-Pressure Decorative Laminates.
  D. ANSI/BHMA A156.9 Cabinet Hardware.
  E. 2010 ADA Standards for Accessible Design and the Texas Accessibility Standards (TAS).

1.3 SUBMITTALS
  A. Shop drawings: Plans, elevations and sections for each casework run, showing dimensions, materials, hardware locations, blocking requirements and connections to adjacent work.
  B. Samples: Plastic laminate, edgebanding and countertop samples for color selection and approval.
  C. Product data: Manufacturer data for core materials, laminates, hardware and countertop materials.

1.4 QUALITY ASSURANCE
  A. Fabricator qualifications: A firm that produces and installs commercial casework of the type specified, with in-house shop drawings, CNC fabrication and installation by its own crews.
  B. Quality standard: Comply with AWS for the grade specified. Where no grade is indicated, provide [Custom] grade.

1.5 DELIVERY, STORAGE AND HANDLING
  A. Do not deliver casework until the building is enclosed, wet work is complete and HVAC is operating to maintain conditions within AWS recommendations.
  B. Protect finished surfaces from damage until Substantial Completion.

1.6 FIELD CONDITIONS
  A. Field measurements: Verify dimensions by field measurement before fabrication and indicate them on shop drawings.

2.6 FABRICATION
  A. Fabricate casework to dimensions, profiles and details indicated on the approved shop drawings, using CNC machining for parts, line boring and hardware locations.
  B. Accessible casework: Provide counter heights, knee clearances and reach ranges complying with the 2010 ADA Standards and TAS where indicated.

PART 3 – EXECUTION

3.1 EXAMINATION
  A. Examine walls, floors and in-wall blocking for conditions affecting installation. Proceed only after unsatisfactory conditions have been corrected.

3.2 INSTALLATION
  A. Install casework level, plumb and true, shimmed as required, and scribed to adjacent surfaces.
  B. Fasten cabinets to in-wall blocking and to adjacent units. Install countertops with concealed fasteners.
  C. Adjust doors, drawers and hardware to operate smoothly.

3.3 CLEANING AND PROTECTION
  A. Clean casework, countertops and hardware. Protect installed work until Substantial Completion, and complete punch list items before turnover.
