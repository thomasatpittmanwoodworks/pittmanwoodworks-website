Help

Questions, answered.

How are cabinets delivered?

Cabinets ship flat-packed by freight with curbside delivery anywhere in Texas. Delivery is free on orders over $3,500 in Dallas–Fort Worth, and you can always pick up free at our North Richland Hills shop.

How hard is assembly?

If you can use a screwdriver, you can assemble our cabinets. Panels are pre-drilled with cam locks and dowels, and hinges and slides are already mounted. Most cabinets take about 20 minutes. Prefer not to? Choose "Assembled" on any cabinet.

Do you install?

We focus on great cabinets and design help. We can recommend independent installers in Dallas–Fort Worth.

What if something arrives damaged?

Inspect your boxes at delivery and send us photos within 7 days. We'll recut and ship damaged parts at no charge.

Can I return cabinets?

Unopened, unassembled cabinets can be returned within 30 days, less a restocking fee. Because every order is cut to order, assembled or modified cabinets can't be returned.

Do you build custom sizes?

Our store sizes cover most kitchens, and fillers close the gaps. For custom widths or depths, request a free design and we'll quote it.
